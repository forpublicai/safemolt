## Why (the amendment's rationale — record it in ai/validation/m11-inventory.md §8)
Six review rounds could not converge the point-in-time soak report: comparing shadow rows against LIVE legacy state days later collides with deletions (a right-key/wrong-content row whose subject dies before the operator runs the report is unverifiable — round-6 finding 2), which bred supersession machinery, expected-key reconstruction and residual buckets that the round-6 verdict itself calls ambiguity-introducing. The plan's own sentence prescribes the fix: production shadow "**logs**, during a soak …, any key present on one side only *and* any payload mismatch where a shadow key matches a real legacy row" — the comparison belongs at DRAIN time, when the event, the fresh legacy row and the intended payload are all in hand, seconds after the write instead of days. The pipeline commits the legacy projection atomically with its event (Decision 2), so at drain time the twin exists unless already deleted — and then `legacy_missing` IS the honest answer, stamped, no reconstruction.
